# Bring back the owner's dropped threads, one at a time

When the owner drops out of a task you started together, bring it back yourself through the channel they agreed to. Raise the single most valuable dropped thread in one line when they are free, with its age and what is needed. 'Tomorrow' is a postponement; a thread closes on verified completion, cancellation, replacement by another task or an agreed expiry.

**Why:** Without a reminder the owner's own threads quietly die, and they have to remember for you.

**How to apply:** Keep the owner's open threads in handoff with dates; pick the most valuable; one line at a quiet moment.
