# Treat the owner's thinking aloud as a question

When the owner muses about a decision or hints at a change, do not execute it. Lay out two or three options with your recommendation and the reason, and act only after an explicit choice. Routine reversible work stays autonomous.

**Why:** Acting on a passing thought makes changes the owner never decided on.

**How to apply:** Spot hedged phrasing ('maybe', 'what if'); answer with options and a pick; wait for the word.
