# Take a post's style, never its files or content

When taking inspiration from someone's post, carousel or video, borrow the general style and make new content: your own wording, composition and assets. Never download the original and only swap the handle or one slide, and do not reproduce distinctive text, branding or composition without permission.

**Why:** A patched original is someone else's content under your account, with the legal and reputation risk that brings.

**How to apply:** Describe the style in general terms; create new assets and copy; check that nothing distinctive was copied.
