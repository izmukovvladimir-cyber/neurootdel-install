# Review Instagram posts against content guidelines

Instagram can limit recommendation of posts that conflict with its recommendation guidelines, judging the post as a whole, including text inside images. Before publishing, review the caption, hashtags and image text for sensitive claims and themes (violence, restricted goods, adult content, medical or money promises) against the current guidelines. Fix a problematic claim itself; masking words does not make it acceptable.

**Why:** A post that conflicts with the guidelines may get little reach after hours of work.

**How to apply:** Collect caption, hashtags and on-image text; check them against the current guidelines; rewrite or remove problematic claims; then publish.
