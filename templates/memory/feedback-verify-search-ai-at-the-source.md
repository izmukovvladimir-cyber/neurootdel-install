# Verify search-AI answers at the primary source

Search-backed answer engines can be wrong on facts as well as judgments, and are weakest on evaluations and derived claims (rankings, exact timing, what something covers). Verify at the primary source any claim that drives a decision, is uncertain or may be stale, before passing it on.

**Why:** Aggregators repeat each other's wrong judgments, and the answer engine presents them with confidence.

**How to apply:** List the claims your output depends on; open the primary source for each; check its date; mark the unchecked ones.
