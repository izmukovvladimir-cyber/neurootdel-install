# On a garbled message, look at what actually arrived

When a person says your message looks wrong, look at the copy they received (ask them to quote it) before rewriting. Secret-masking filters on the delivery path can replace the middle of long strings without spaces, including links. If the masked string is a secret, the filter did its job and it is never resent or split to get past it.

**Why:** Rewriting your text cannot fix a mangling that happens after you send it.

**How to apply:** Ask for a quote or screenshot; compare with what you sent; if a filter masked a non-secret, deliver it through an approved route such as a file; never split a secret to bypass masking.
