# Plan Instagram Direct inside the 24-hour window

Through the Instagram messaging API a business can generally reply only within 24 hours of the person's last message, apart from message types the policy permits. If sends start failing with a restriction, stop automated sending and follow the error or notice instead of retrying.

**Why:** Follow-ups outside the window fail, and retrying into a restriction can make it worse.

**How to apply:** Check the current messaging policy of your integration; keep follow-ups inside the window; on a restriction error pause sends and read the reason.
