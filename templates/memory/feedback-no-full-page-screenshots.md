# Do not feed full-page screenshots into a turn

A screenshot of a long page can exceed the model's image size limits, so the request may fail or the image may be downscaled until unreadable. Capture the relevant region at normal size, or work from source files.

**Why:** An oversized image can waste the step and hide the detail you needed.

**How to apply:** Screenshot a viewport or element; resize before reading; prefer source files or text for long pages.
