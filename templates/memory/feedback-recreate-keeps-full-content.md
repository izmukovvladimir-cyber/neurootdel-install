# When recreating a piece, keep all of its content

Recreating, translating or reskinning a source piece keeps its topic, hook and full content, and its structure unless the brief says otherwise. Diff it against the source element by element before sending, and ask about any divergence instead of substituting your own content.

**Why:** Shortened or paraphrased recreations lose the parts that made the original work, and the owner has to request it again.

**How to apply:** Open each source element; carry it over in full; compare side by side; add only what the brief asks for.
