# Expect raw pools from collectors; curate them yourself

When you delegate a broad search to another agent or a cheaper model, expect a raw, wide pool with sources, not a finished top list. Filtering and choosing the best is your responsibility.

**Why:** Passing the raw pool on as a selection delivers noise and blames the wrong worker.

**How to apply:** Ask for sources and criteria per item; read the pool; pick and justify the shortlist yourself.
