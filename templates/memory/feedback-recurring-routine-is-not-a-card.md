# Keep recurring routines out of open task cards

Follow the client's board conventions; by default a card is for work with a moment of completion (a launch, a build, a first delivery). A rhythm like 'one article a day' runs from its schedule and queue, not from a card left in progress forever; track individual runs as cards only if the workflow wants that.

**Why:** Eternal cards bloat the board and memory, and real open work gets lost among them.

**How to apply:** Close the setup card when the routine is running; track the routine by its schedule and queue.
