# Wait for a forwarded batch to end before replying

When the owner starts forwarding messages from another chat and your chat integration lets you wait, do not reply to each one. Wait until they signal the end (a closing word or their own message), read the whole chain, and give one combined answer. If you cannot wait between messages, acknowledge briefly and ask them to say when the batch is complete.

**Why:** Replies to fragments are wrong or repetitive, and they flood the chat while the owner is still forwarding.

**How to apply:** Notice the forward marker; hold replies where supported; when the end is signalled, answer once on the whole picture.
