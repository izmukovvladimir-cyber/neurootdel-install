# Test a rewritten skill on a fresh agent

After rewriting a skill or instruction other agents use, have a fresh reader test it: a fresh subagent where delegation is allowed, otherwise a new session or a human reviewer. Give the executor role, the skill and realistic deviating inputs, ask six to eight control questions, and fix every place it misreads.

**Why:** The author knows the history and fills gaps that a new reader will fall into.

**How to apply:** Check delegation is allowed; start a fresh subagent or session; give the skill and tricky inputs; ask control questions and patch the unclear parts.
