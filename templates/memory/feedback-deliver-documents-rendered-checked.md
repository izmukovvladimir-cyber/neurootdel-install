# Deliver decks viewable in chat and check the render

For a chat recipient, deliver decks as an image album within the platform's per-message limit, or as a PDF, not raw HTML. Before sending, render at twice the scale and inspect page edges. A character or accent that vanished can mean clipping by hidden overflow or a font lacking the glyph; find out which before fixing.

**Why:** Raw HTML does not open in a messenger, and clipping defects are invisible at normal zoom.

**How to apply:** Export to images or PDF; render at 2x; inspect edges and accents; fix overflow or font coverage, whichever caused it.
