# Check back on channels that do not wake you

If you hand a task to someone through a channel with no wake-up trigger for you (a user-account chat, a mailbox, a third-party bot), their reply will sit silently. Check that channel yourself at a set interval and write the check into handoff.

**Why:** The other side answered long ago while you waited, and the task stalled on your side.

**How to apply:** Note the channel and expected reply time in handoff; check it on schedule; act on the reply.
